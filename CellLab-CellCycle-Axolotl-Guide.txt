Cell Life Cycle · Axolotl guide

Voice: free local Kokoro, af_heart, matching the other CellLab lessons.
Opening: CellCycleNarration.overview in CellCycleNarration.swift.
Starts with a neuron fun fact about G₀ and explains that neurons are still
active even while they are not dividing.

The phase recordings use the displayed title, subtitle, description, bullets,
and checkpoint from CellCycleContent.swift. Mitosis follows its selected
stage, and cytokinesis follows the animal/plant comparison. The key-player
recordings read the displayed title, caption, and definitions.

Tap the axolotl to hear the opening and the selected phase. Select another
phase to hear it. Stop cancels both the current and queued recording.
Audio stops on leaving the page, opening the key-player sheet, or backgrounding
the app. The sheet has its own axolotl that follows the selected key player.
All recordings are saved in the app bundle and work offline.

Regenerate using only the installed local voice and model:
Tools/.kokoro-venv/bin/python Tools/generate_cell_cycle_audio.py

Pronunciation: G₀ = G zero, G₁ = G one, G₂ = G two, S = ess, M = em,
p53 = p fifty three. Preserve the shared DNA, RNA, nuclei, copies,
nucleotide, and mitosis-stage pronunciation fixes. Ordinary A is an article.
