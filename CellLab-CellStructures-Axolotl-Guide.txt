Use the same af_heart voice, warm and playful, as the other CellLab lessons.
Pause after the opening question. The overview introduces the model and how
to explore it, then hands off to the selected structure (initially Nucleus).
After each explanation, wait for the learner to choose another structure.

CellStructuresNarration.swift contains the opening and builds every clip
from the existing CellStructuresContent.swift text. The main explanation
reads the structure title, nickname, summary, and animation caption.
“Look closer” audio reads the same detailed bullet points shown on screen.
No duplicate organelle descriptions are maintained in this document.

Say DNA, RNA, mRNA, rRNA, ATP, and ER clearly. Keep the existing copied,
copies, nuclei, nucleotide, and RNA pronunciation fixes. Expand chemical
notation for speech: O₂ as oxygen, CO₂ as carbon dioxide, H₂O as water,
H₂O₂ as hydrogen peroxide, and Ca²⁺ as calcium. Read pH 4.8 as
“P H four point eight,” nm as nanometers, and + as plus. The displayed
transcripts retain the original scientific notation.

The guide is quiet until tapped. Its mouth moves while speaking, with
Reduce Motion respected. Tapping Stop cancels speech and pending clips.
Choosing a structure follows the selection while guided narration is on;
opening the structure browser, leaving, or backgrounding stops the audio.
