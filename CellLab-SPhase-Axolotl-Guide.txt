RECORD lesson.overview | Opening · Listen
How do you share a whole instruction book without tearing it in half? Make another copy!

That’s what a cell does with its DNA before it divides. Welcome to S phase, the synthesis phase of interphase. This is when the cell makes another complete set of its DNA instructions.

Watch our little zipper: that’s helicase, opening the DNA so each original strand can guide a new one. A whole crew helps build, check, and finish the new strands. The result? Two matching DNA molecules, each with one original strand and one new strand. That’s DNA replication!

We’re getting the DNA ready, before the cell divides. Swipe through the nine steps with me!
