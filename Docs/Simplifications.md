# Simplifications

- Transcription prose describes human nuclear protein-coding gene transcription and is not a complete account of every type of transcription.
- The synthetic activity starts during elongation with an already-transcribed RNA prefix. Its DNA bases are invented, not a real human gene; the initial RNA is derived from the stored prefix.
- Coordinates are zero-based, half-open offsets into the stored template sequence.
- This milestone supports only the declared 3′ → 5′ stored template orientation. Other orientations are rejected until their handling is deliberately specified.
- “Excerpt completed” means the playable range ended. It does not mean transcription terminated.
- Incorrect precursor choices leave the RNA and template position unchanged. No animation, collision, persistence, or initiation behavior is part of the transcription rules.
