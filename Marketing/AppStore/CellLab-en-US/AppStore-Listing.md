## Description — 1585 / 4,000 characters

Biology makes more sense when you can move it around.

CellLab is a pocket-sized biology lab for curious minds and Bio 101 students. Build a DNA strand, explore an animal cell, and follow chromosomes through division, with a friendly axolotl to talk you through the science.

BUILD DNA, BASE BY BASE
Unzip DNA with helicase, match complementary nucleotides, catch a mismatch, and seal the backbone. See how the molecular team works together as you build.

MEET THE CELL'S TINY TEAM
Tap organelles in an animal cell to explore what they do. Connect each structure to its job with clear illustrations, short definitions, and closer looks.

FOLLOW THE CHROMOSOMES
Step through mitosis and meiosis with visual phase cards. See chromosomes line up and separate, then put the ideas into practice with hands-on games.

BRING A FRIEND TO THE LAB
Tap the axolotl for recorded overviews, explanations, definitions, and game instructions. Listen at your own pace and stop or replay when you need to.

CONNECT THE BIG IDEAS
Explore the cell life cycle, DNA replication, DNA to RNA to protein, cellular respiration, and DNA technology. Follow a short explanation or spend time with the details.

KEEP YOUR CURIOSITY CLOSE
Add the small or medium Daily Biology Fact widget to your Home Screen for a new discovery each day. Tap it to jump into CellLab.

For a study break, a refresher, or the question that sends you down a biology rabbit hole, there's always another part of the lab to explore.

Lessons, games, and recorded guides work offline. External reference links need an internet connection.
