Capture provenance
- iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.2: actual DNA replication gameplay, organelle
  selection, metaphase/anaphase cards, speaking axolotl, medium and small
  placed widgets, and widget taps back to CellLab.
- iPad Pro 13-inch (M5), iPadOS 26.2: separate real captures and the same
  six passing feature checks. iPad assets are not resized iPhone images.
- All app interaction was through normal controls; no injected game state
  or synthesized interface. The large axolotl is rendered from the app's
  existing SwiftUI vector drawing. Typography/layout uses native macOS
  AppKit/CoreGraphics. No generated artwork or paid image API was used.
- Previews/Capture-Crops.json records source and destination rectangles.
  Intentional close-ups preserve aspect ratio, labels, strand directions,
  base pairing, phase titles, and the simplified chromosome-count note.
- Only relevant widget bounds were cropped from actual SpringBoard PNGs,
  keeping unrelated app icons and the test-runner icon out of final images.

Supporting tools
Tools/AppStoreCaptureUITests.swift preserves the capture flow, outside all
shipping targets. A temporary isolated Xcode UI-test harness compiled it
without re-scanning the project's large cloud-backed audio library. The
SpringBoard-only idle-wait workaround in this helper is capture automation
and is not linked into the shipped app. Tools/RenderOpening.swift preserves
the A/B layouts. Tools/RenderLaunchKit.swift and VerifyLaunchKit.swift
reproduce and validate the final layouts when compiled on macOS and run
from this en-US directory.
