CellLab — universal creative asset

UPLOAD THIS FILE
CellLab-Universal-Creative-5244x2950.png

Use it for both the product-page header and search-results creative.
It is separate from the five numbered iPhone/iPad screenshots.

App Store Connect > CellLab > app version > Product Page Information >
Header and Search Results > Header: upload the universal PNG.
Enable "Use header asset in search results" for the matching search placement.
Choose Preview to inspect iPhone/iPad, portrait/landscape, before saving
and submitting. Alternatively, upload through Asset Library > + >
Upload Creative Assets and select it for these placements afterward.

SPECIFICATIONS AND CHECKS
5244 x 2950 pixels, sRGB PNG, no alpha channel or transparency.
Apple's universal art safe area is x:1921, y:660, width:1402, height:962.
The title, tagline, and existing CellLab vector axolotl fit wholly inside
that rectangle. Background circles extend across the auxiliary area.
Size, RGB color, opacity, safe-area containment, and text recognition were
checked locally; Validation.json records the results.

Preview-Safe-Area.png shows the protected central artwork enlarged.
Preview-Placement-Guide.png marks the safe area on the complete canvas.
These previews are for checking the design, not for upload. They are local
layout checks; the actual App Store device/orientation previews still need
to be checked with Preview in your App Store Connect account.

Creative asset placements are available in the App Store on iOS/iPadOS 27
and later. This marketing asset does not change the app's minimum OS.
