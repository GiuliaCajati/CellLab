# Details to supply in App Store Connect

The images and listing copy are ready. These account and ownership details still need you:

- **Support URL:** A public page with a way to contact you. Apple requires an actual support/contact destination; a placeholder URL is insufficient.
- **Privacy policy and App Privacy answers:** Supply a public privacy-policy URL and confirm what the released app and any third-party services collect. I found bundled lesson/audio/fact content, on-device storage, local notifications, and external reference links; that inspection does not certify your privacy answers. I did not find a public in-app privacy-policy link in the inspected build, so arrange an accessible in-app link before submission.
- **Copyright and App Review contact:** Your copyright owner/year and reviewer contact name, email, and phone number. Confirm commercial rights to the included artwork, recordings, and other content when answering Content Rights.
- **Launch choices:** Confirm name availability, category, price, countries/regions, release timing, and age-rating questionnaire answers. Suggested primary category: Education. No price or age-rating claim was added to the marketing images.
- **Submission build:** Select your signed iOS Release build and complete any account-specific agreements and export-compliance questions. The Simulator app used for these captures is not an App Store upload binary.

A marketing website URL is optional. No new server, account system, or push-notification setup is needed for the feature images in this kit.

References: [Apple's version fields and required support URL](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information/), [Apple's app information and privacy-policy URL](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-and-compliance-information), [App Privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/).
